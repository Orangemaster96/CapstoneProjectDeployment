Organize your courses, track assessments, and manage your academic schedule.

About the App

Student Mobile App is an academic planner that helps students manage their academic terms, courses, and assessments. Keep important course information together, save study notes, and set reminders for upcoming dates.

Features

  • Create and manage academic terms and courses.
  • Track course dates, status, and instructor contact information.
  • Manage objective and performance assessments.
  • Save and share course notes.
  • Choose reminder times for course and assessment start and end dates.
  • Search saved records, notes, and instructor information.
  • Generate basic reports for terms, course statuses, and upcoming assessments.
